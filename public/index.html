⚠ Hazardous waste rules

Keep hazardous materials separate, in original containers where safe. Never burn, puncture, mix, pour into drains or put into ordinary bins. Contact an authorised collection point or local authority.

♻ Sorting principles

Empty, rinse if needed, dry, and separate mixed components. Recycling acceptance differs between municipalities and facilities. Always verify local rules.
